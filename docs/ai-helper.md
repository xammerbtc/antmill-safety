# How the AntMill AI helper works

The AntMill helper is a free educational assistant. It answers questions about common scams, wallet hygiene, and risky patterns people see in Telegram and the wider crypto internet.

It is not a trading bot, not a portfolio manager, and not a human support desk.

## Where to use it

- Telegram: [@antmill_poster_bot](https://t.me/antmill_poster_bot)
- Web, with no sign-up: [antmill-media.pages.dev](https://antmill-media.pages.dev/) (AI page)

The Colony Mini App is opened from the same Telegram bot. It is a learning app: lessons, basic risk checks, questions, and invites. It is not a place to deposit funds.

## Model and hosting

Answers are generated with **Llama 3.1 8B** through **Cloudflare Workers AI**.

That is a compact, general-purpose language model. It can miss things, sound confident when it is unsure, and it cannot see your wallet or the private state of another app. Treat it as a second opinion, not as an authority.

## What the helper will do

- Explain common scam patterns in plain language.
- Help you think through a suspicious message, username, or claim.
- Point you to safer habits (see the [anti-scam guide](anti-scam-guide.md) and the [checklist](wallet-account-checklist.md)).
- Stay within education. It should not tell you that a coin, a trade, or a launch will make money.

## What the helper will not do

- Ask for a seed phrase, private key, or backup.
- Ask you to connect a funded wallet in a DM.
- Promise returns, “guaranteed recovery”, or that a token will succeed.
- Give legal, tax, or partnership commitments. Questions about refunds, complaints, legal matters, or business deals are routed to the human team.
- Speak for a “kids” product line. This project is crypto-safety education for a general audience.

If the bot ever asked for a seed phrase, that would not be us. Stop and report it.
